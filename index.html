A welcoming community is something we create together. These values help everyone feel safer, seen and respected.

A few helpful things

Good to know before you join.

Who is this community for?

Desires & Pleasures DP welcomes open-minded adults, including couples, single adults and LGBTQ+ individuals, who value respect and consent. Please contact the host for specific participation guidelines.

Is there an age requirement?

Yes. This is an adults-only community for people aged 18 and over.

How can I find out about events?

Use the contact section to reach the host and ask about upcoming gatherings, dates, locations and attendance details.

Where can I find contribution details?

Contribution amounts and any event-specific arrangements should be confirmed directly with the host so you always receive current information.
