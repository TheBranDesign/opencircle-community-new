The BalCal Construction Process

A disciplined four-phase methodology aligning budget, architectural vision, and precise site execution.

Turnkey General Contracting
01
Phase 01

Pre-Construction & Fiscal Alignment

We conduct comprehensive site feasibility, evaluate existing structures, and establish transparent line-item budgets. Cruz Calderon and our pre-construction team ensure fiscal responsibility is baked into the foundation before ground is broken.

Site Analysis Budget Engineering Permit Roadmapping
02
Phase 02

Architectural & Program Coordination

Max Ballines leads close collaboration with your architect, structural engineers, and interior designers. We translate blueprint intent into construction reality, harmonizing mechanical, structural, and aesthetic systems.

Architect Collaboration Subcontractor Vetting Material Sourcing
03
Phase 03

Precision Execution & Site Leadership

Daily superintendent oversight, uncompromising safety protocols, and eco-conscious waste management. We maintain spotless jobsites, adhere strictly to project milestones, and provide regular, clear progress briefings.

Daily Jobsite Oversight Rigid Safety Compliance Eco Recycling Methods

Phase 04

Our in-house master craftsmen execute bespoke millwork, architectural joinery, and fine surfaces. We perform exhaustive punch-list inspections, deliver complete documentation, and provide lasting post-construction support.
